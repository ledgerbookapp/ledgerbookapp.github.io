Why this one

Private by design, correct by design

Where your data goes, whether the totals hold, and whether it needs a network at all.

It has nowhere to send your money data

Not a promise — a fact about how it is built. Two files in the whole app touch the internet, and both of them are the thing that checks whether a new version exists. There is no server of ours for an entry to go to. Android’s own backup is a separate matter, and the privacy page says exactly what it carries.

Every amount is written twice

Real double-entry, the way an accountant keeps books: money leaving one place always arrives somewhere. The Trial Balance page shows the two sides agreeing, so a wrong total is caught instead of carried.

It works with the phone in flight mode

Nothing waits on a network. The ledger is encrypted on the phone, backups are encrypted files you keep yourself, and the app locks behind a PIN or your fingerprint.

Questions

Before you install

My phone warned me. Is it safe?

Android shows that warning for every app that does not come from the Play Store, because it cannot tell one unknown file from another. Play Protect then scans this one and says it looks safe. To be sure the file is exactly the one published, the install guide shows how to check its SHA-256 fingerprint.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free, with no ads and no account to open.

What if I change or lose my phone?

Your ledger is encrypted on this phone, so it does not move to a new one on its own. Before you change phones, save an encrypted backup file from the app’s Backup page, restore it on the new phone and set a new PIN there. Keep a copy of that file off the phone too, in case the phone is lost.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. It goes online only to check for a newer version, and to download it when you say yes.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. It is for Android only, version 6.0 and up.

How do I get updates?

The app tells you when a new version is out, downloads it when you say yes, and installs it. You do not need to come back to this page.
