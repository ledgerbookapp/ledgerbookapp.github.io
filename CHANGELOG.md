# Website versions

The website's own version, shown in every footer as "Website vX.Y.Z" (SITE_VERSION in
`assets/site.js`). It has nothing to do with the app's version.

- **Major** — the site rebuilt or redesigned as a whole.
- **Minor** — a new page, section or feature.
- **Patch** — a fix, a retaken picture, a measurement held to the drawing.

Counted back over the history when the number was introduced; repository housekeeping
(the first commit, taking in the repository's history) carries no number.
